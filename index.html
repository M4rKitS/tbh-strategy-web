About Tamara

Hi, I'm Tamara, the face behind TBH Strategy.

I'm a social media manager and content creator based in Tralee, Co. Kerry, working with small businesses who are brilliant at what they do, but don't have the time or headspace to keep up with their social media.

I studied Social Media for Digital Marketing at Kerry College, and I'm always learning — from SEO and AEO courses to how businesses get discovered through AI search. What I love most isn't the likes or follower counts, it's seeing the real impact: more enquiries, more people recognizing the brand, more confidence from business owners knowing their online presence is working.

What I Can Do For You

Content Creation

Photos, captions, reels and carousels, all created in your brand's voice, for your audience. Professional, consistent content that makes your business look as good online as it is in real life.

Social Media Strategy

A clear, tailored plan built around your business. You'll know exactly what to post, when, where and why. So you can show up with purpose.

Content Planning & Scheduling

Your content mapped out and scheduled in advance, so your platforms stay active while you're flat out running your business.

Account Review & Audit

Already on social media but not seeing results? Not enough traffic to your website? I'll take an honest look at what's working, what isn't, and exactly what needs to change.

That's the full picture — designed specifically for small businesses in Kerry who want their social media to turn followers into customers and drive real traffic to their website.
